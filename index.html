Our Roots & Passion

The Legacy of Kisa Khani

Named after the world-renowned Qissa Khwani Bazaar (The Bazaar of Storytellers), our kitchen brings centuries of culinary mastery to Rawalpindi.

The Art of Kabab-Making

"Kisa Khani Kabab invites you to savor the art of kabab-making. Our menu features a delectable array of traditional and innovative kababs, expertly grilled to perfection along with breakfast."
