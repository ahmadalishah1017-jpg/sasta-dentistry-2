About the Clinic

Honest Dentistry, Higher Standards.

Sasti Dentistry was founded on a simple belief — that quality dental care should never be a luxury. Our clinic on Range Road, opposite Sonari Bank in Shalley Valley, brings clinical precision and genuine warmth to every patient who walks through the door.

Every instrument is autoclave-sterilised, every treatment plan is explained before it begins, and every price is quoted upfront. No surprises — just careful, unhurried dentistry.

  • Transparent, upfront pricing on every procedure
  • Fully sterilised, single-use disposables
  • Gentle, pain-managed treatment approach
  • Walk-ins and same-day emergencies welcome
